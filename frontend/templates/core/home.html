How It Works

Simple steps that make a big difference.

01
Donor Registration
Donor creates an account, submits personal & medical details, and provides digital consent for organ pledge.
02
Hospital Verification
Partner hospital reviews donor eligibility, performs medical assessment, and approves the donation.
03
Organ Availability
Verified organs are listed in the platform inventory, visible to all authorized hospitals in the network.
04
Recipient Matching
System matches compatible recipients based on blood type, tissue compatibility, urgency, and proximity.
05
Blockchain Recording
Every transaction — consent, verification, match, transfer — is immutably recorded on the Ethereum blockchain.
06
Transplant Completion
Transplant outcome is logged, case is closed, and the complete audit trail is finalized on the ledger.

Technology

Why Blockchain?

Blockchain infrastructure ensures that every organ donation record is tamper-proof, auditable, and transparent.

Immutable Records

Once a transaction is recorded on the blockchain, it cannot be altered or deleted — ensuring data integrity.

Transparency

All authorized stakeholders can independently verify every step of the organ donation process in real time.

Security

Cryptographic hashing protects patient data while maintaining verifiability for authorized medical personnel.

Traceability

Complete chain of custody from donor consent to transplant outcome — every handoff is permanently logged.

Multi-Hospital Collaboration

Decentralized architecture enables secure organ sharing between hospitals without a single point of failure.

Audit Trail

Comprehensive, time-stamped logs of every action — from registration to transplant — for regulatory compliance.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Every day, thousands of patients are added to national transplant waiting lists. Organ donation is critical because the demand for healthy organs far exceeds the available supply. A single deceased donor can save up to 8 lives and enhance the lives of over 75 others, making it one of the most impactful medical contributions possible.
Organs can be donated either during a person's life (living donation) or after they have passed away (deceased donation). Living donors can typically donate one kidney, a lung lobe, or a portion of the liver. Deceased donation occurs only after all life-saving efforts have been exhausted and clinical death is strictly confirmed by independent physicians.
Anyone can register their intent to donate regardless of age or medical history! For living donors, you must be a healthy adult (usually over 18). For deceased donors, strict medical suitability is evaluated at the time of death. Very few medical conditions automatically disqualify you from donating.
In living donation, you can absolutely choose to be a "directed donor" to give an organ directly to a specific family member or friend. In deceased donation, however, organs are allocated based on strict, blind medical criteria (blood type, urgency, time on waitlist) to ensure fairness across the national network.
Blockchain creates a decentralized, immutable ledger of every transaction. From the moment you register your consent to the final transplant surgery, every step is cryptographically hashed and recorded. This guarantees that donor consent cannot be altered, prevents medical data manipulation, and ensures complete, transparent traceability for authorized hospitals.
We never store raw patient data directly on the blockchain. Instead, personal medical records are kept in secure, encrypted hospital databases. The blockchain only stores "hashes" (cryptographic fingerprints) of the data. This allows the system to verify that the data is authentic without exposing your private medical history to the public.
